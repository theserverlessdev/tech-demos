What this shows Clef-omni reads a photo, a voice note, or a short video in one request and returns a probability for each allowed answer. There is no transcribe-then-classify chain unless you ask for the comparison.

What you can do

  • Snap a photo, record a voice note, or film up to 10 seconds.
  • Or start from a small self-made sample.
  • Pick a preset checklist, or write up to five questions with two to six options each.
  • Read the top answer, the bar for every option, and the latency.
  • Compare that with Whisper or a vision model, then Clef, on the same image or audio.
  • Look back at recent judgments from this browser.

Media you upload is deleted after 24 hours. Model text is untrusted.
