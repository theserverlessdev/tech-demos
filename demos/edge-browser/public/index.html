Edge browser

Headless Chrome, one URL at a time

Contact

What this shows A real headless Chrome runs at the edge from a Worker on the Workers Paid plan. Paste a URL. A Workflow saves a desktop screenshot, a 390px phone screenshot, a PDF, and markdown, then Workers AI writes a short summary.

A cron deletes capture rows and their R2 files about a day after they are created. It runs every 15 minutes, so a file can remain a little longer. An R2 lifecycle rule is the backstop. No account.

What you can do

  • Paste a URL, pass Turnstile, and watch each workflow step.
  • Scroll the desktop and phone screenshots inside the frame.
  • Open the PDF, read the markdown, and read the summary.
  • See whether the capture reused the warm browser or launched a new one.
  • Try a blocked address, such as loopback or a metadata host, and read the rejection.
