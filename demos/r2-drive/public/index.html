R2 Drive

Report misuse

A short-lived sandbox for testing WebDAV with small documents. This demo turns R2 into a WebDAV Class 1 and Class 2 drive with locks kept in a SQLite Durable Object. Only text, PDF and office documents are accepted, and the prefix is wiped within an hour.

What this shows

PROPFIND, LOCK, and PUT against an isolated R2 prefix. The lock owner, timeout, and depth stay live. A cron, a Durable Object alarm, and a one-day R2 lifecycle rule delete what the hour does not.

What you can try

Verify an email, accept the notice, and mount the drive from macOS, Windows, rclone, or Obsidian. Or use the browser to upload, rename, move, delete, and preview a text or PDF file.

What you could build

A personal Dropbox-style drive, an Obsidian or Zotero sync target, or a backup destination for rclone. Keep the same prefix isolation and give it a longer, authenticated life.
